NARUTO CUSTOM CARD GAME — COMPLETE RULES FOUNDATIONS & APPROVED AKATSUKI CARDS
🧩 SECTION I — CORE RULES FOUNDATIONS
🌀 1. Card Types
Every card belongs to one of the following categories:
•	Character Cards
•	Jutsu Cards
•	Item Cards
•	Mission Cards
•	Terrain Cards
•	Token Cards
🛡️ 2. Character Role Types (Max 2 per character)
A character may have up to two of the following:
•	Vanguard — tanking, prevention, durability
•	Assault — damage, aggression
•	Support — healing, buffs, utility
•	Tactician — battlefield control, tempo manipulation, denial
No character may have more than two role types.
⚡ 3. Play Speed Rules
Two speeds exist:
Instant
•	Can be played on any turn
•	Used for reactions, counters, defenses, quick techniques
Sorcery
•	Can only be played on your turn
•	Used for attacks, summons, rituals, transformations
🔥 4. Chakra Rules
Base Chakra Gain
At the start of each of your turns:
You gain 1 Chakra.
Face Down Chakra Rule
Once per turn, during your main phase:
You may place 1 card from your hand face down as Chakra.
Face down Chakra:
•	Produces 1 Chakra per turn
•	Has no text
•	Cannot be interacted with unless an ability explicitly references Chakra
•	Is not a character, item, terrain, or mission
This is your Naruto flavored mana system.
🧬 5. Styles (Correct Definition)
Styles refer only to elemental or kekkei genkai classifications.
Valid Styles
•	Fire Style
•	Water Style
•	Wind Style
•	Earth Style
•	Lightning Style
•	Explosion Style (kekkei genkai)
•	Ice Style (optional)
•	Lava Style (optional)
•	Boil Style (optional)
•	Gravity Style (special case: Pain)
❌ NOT Styles (These are Ability Types)
•	Ritual
•	Scythe
•	Taijutsu
•	Kenjutsu
•	Puppet
•	Curse
•	Summoning
•	Sealing
These do not appear in the Style field.
🛠️ 6. Ability Types
Ability Types describe how the ability functions, not its elemental nature.
Examples:
•	Ritual Ability
•	Scythe Ability
•	Explosion Ability
•	Water Ability
•	Gravity Ability
•	Puppet Ability
•	Curse Ability
These do not impose restrictions — they simply inform flavor.
🛡️ 7. Synergy Rules
Synergy is a tag, not a restriction.
•	Characters with the same Synergy may have bonus interactions
•	Synergy is never required to play abilities
•	Akatsuki synergy is mostly flavor
🧠 8. Rank System
Ranks determine power scaling and interaction rules.
Rank Hierarchy
•	C Rank — basic shinobi
•	B Rank — chunin / mid tier
•	A Rank — elite jonin
•	S Rank — legendary shinobi
•	SS Rank — transformation tier
•	SSS Rank — ultimate transformation tier
Rank Effects
•	Chakra steal (Kisame steals more from lower ranks)
•	Chakra drain (Pain drains from lower ranks)
•	Healing bonuses (Kisame heals more vs lower ranks)
•	Forbidden Techniques often require S or SS rank
🧱 9. Jutsu Rules
Cost Philosophy
•	Costs reflect impact, setup, and speed
•	Cheap abilities build toward expensive ultimates
•	Forbidden Techniques cost 6–7 Chakra and require conditions
Conditional Mechanics
Conditions must be:
•	Flavor accurate
•	Mechanically meaningful
•	Clean and readable
Examples:
•	Hidan must Blood Mark a target
•	Deidara must reach 1 HP for C0
•	Kisame must reach 5 Fusion Counters
•	Pain must respect gravitational cooldown
Forbidden Techniques
•	Extremely powerful
•	Require extreme conditions
•	Cost 6–7 Chakra
•	Often self damaging or self sacrificing
🧱 10. Tokens
Tokens have:
•	HP
•	One ability
•	No chakra pool
•	No traits
•	No rank
•	No synergy
🧱 11. Design Philosophy (Final & Complete)
Flavor Integration
Every Akatsuki member has a signature mechanic:
•	Kakuzu → Hearts
•	Hidan → Ritual
•	Pain → Gravity Rhythm
•	Deidara → Clay Charges
•	Kisame → Fusion Counters
No Ability Restrictions
Abilities do not list “requires X style” or “requires Y clan.” All bonuses are baked into numbers.
Clean, readable, flavorful
Every card must be:
•	Mechanically tight
•	Flavor accurate
•	Easy to parse
•	Unique in identity
